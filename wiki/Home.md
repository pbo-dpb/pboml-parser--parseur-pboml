# PBOML Wiki Home

Welcome to the PBOML wiki!

PBOML (Parliamentary Budget Office Markup Language) is a human-readable data serialization language based on YAML, designed for publishing structured reports and analyses on the PBO website. This wiki provides an overview of the language, its structure, and the different content types supported by PBOML, with examples and explanations for each section.
